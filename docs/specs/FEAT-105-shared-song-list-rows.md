## Required outcome

- Use **one** song-row renderer/configuration in both production hosts, still based on `MusicRow`. Share the minimum lazy row-emission/spacing policy or equivalent that owns Search-canonical song pitch and horizontal alignment, rather than merely pointing out that `MusicRow` was already shared. Avoid host-specific song style overrides, extra wrappers with independent padding/background or configurable style forks.
- For identical song/state and equal available width/font scale/theme, the row surface, art size/shape/type cue, title/credit styling and ellipsis, internal padding, measured height, current/paused/buffering colors/state description, focus/ripple, More/action slots and **consecutive song-row top-to-top pitch** must match Search. Search canonical gap is zero; at normal metrics the current minimum is 72dp, growing consistently at large fonts. Do not add card gutters, borders, shadows or inline metadata/credit buttons.
- Remove the Home-only 12dp **between consecutive recent-song rows** without globally deleting the intentional shelf/card spacing. Keep Home's Liked Songs, Recently played and Your playlists structure, boundary breathing room and playlist cards; keep Search groups/dock/errors/paging unchanged. Both already have 16dp horizontal list insets: share/reuse that budget without double-padding. Do not force Home's entire outer flow to Search's 8dp vertical edge padding.
- Keep individual lazy items and caller-owned stable key namespaces/order. Do not nest a scrollable list, place the whole recent shelf in an eager/non-lazy song `Column`, recreate state holders or shift Search indices with gratuitous spacer/header items. Share visual/interaction emission, not state/data lifetimes. If spacing is made explicit at shelf boundaries, preserve normal scroll/Back continuity and last-row reachability.
- Same item/state must offer the same row destination, named trailing More, long-press More, appropriate custom accessibility actions, like pending/retry gating and **landed** contextual right-swipe affordance/availability in both hosts. Child More must not also navigate/swipe; touch slop, cancellation, at-most-once release, vertical scrolling and edge Back remain intact. Accessible actions must match actual capabilities; full credits/all known IDs remain intentional More/details targets, with missing IDs honest text.
- Do not change row-tap to Play, replace a contextual playback sequence with Search order/recent order, treat visible rows as a complete playback queue, or introduce autoplay on composition/refresh/navigation. Preserve exact item/context/occurrence and stale session/route/action fences through any shared adapter; it must not cache callbacks across hosts or rebind a held action to new data.
- Long/Unicode/many/absent credits, missing art/audio and background refresh must retain the same two-line geometry and More target in both hosts. Unavailable playback actions stay unavailable while navigation/More remain usable; no false loading/empty/success interpretation. Keep shared rendering stable while each host displays its own loading/error/retry context and retained healthy content.
- Final track-row contract has **no inline Play/Pause or reserved playback spacer**, with reclaimed title width; collection header Play and player transport stay. **Lead07 owns delivering that change:** do not duplicate its removal/gesture/FIFO implementation or revive buttons while refactoring. This parity feature can land independently using the row contract then on main; if07 has not landed, report removal as pending07, not delivered by parity.
