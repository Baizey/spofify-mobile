## Required behavior and negative guarantees

- On an already prepared live current occurrence, a fresh admitted **app Resume** and permitted notification/headset/system Resume start that exact occurrence once from retained paused position, preserving complete queue/traversal/order, duplicate keys, contexts, High/Low origins/membership, repeat/shuffle, current event nonce/progress/reported state. No album/playlist/derived refetch, fresh selection token, queue replacement, next-head promotion or duplicate history. Normal paused Resume differs from deliberate ended/Previous traversal restart; preserve the latter's legitimate new-event semantics with exact receipt fencing.
- Pause drains actual audible/buffered output promptly, cancels unresolved preparation/start, relinquishes focus and FGS/notification ownership as appropriate, preserves resumable queue/current/position, and reports authoritative paused state. Focus gain, noisy recovery, heartbeat, late readiness, reopening, controller rebind or permission recovery never resumes automatically. Stale attempt cleanup may drain its own resources but cannot stop a successor start/service.
- Repeat several settled cycles and fast cycles. An accepted success requires actual supported-layer start/progress, not `playWhenReady=true`, a Pause→Play callback count, an optimistic displayed icon or broker POST acceptance. Distinguish desired versus observed state during buffering and honest `blocked`/`media`/rejected/uncertain outcomes. If a click cannot be admitted during Pause publication, expose a clear reachable state/guidance and allow a **fresh** action after settlement; no silent no-op/endless false buffering, no delayed automatic replay of the rejected click. Preserve 48dp controls and music-first UI; no debug panel/redesign.
- Respect key domains: benign position/buffering/duration and same-owner lease renewals must not invalidate a valid held operation. Genuine current/queue/history-event/revision/session/service/output or unsampled A→B→A changes must. A held generic queue/menu action may not acquire a new binding merely because transport recovered. A fresh transport action must not accidentally reuse an old generic/skip key. Null/unavailable is not permission to initialize restored display or weaken all guards.
- Same-owner Pause during changed-current queue start preserves the original accepted count/placement/commitRevision and reports superseded/null playbackKey with actual final paused state where BR-001 permits; it never falsely ACKs Playing or rewrites a frozen receipt. Owner/session loss follows cancellation/uncertainty, not same-owner success.
- **Mandatory counterexample:** A paused at 0:42, pending High H1, Low C1/C2; append Low B1/B2 → `[A,H1,C1,C2,B1,B2]`, A remains current/paused at 0:42, H1 pending, B Low, no prepare/start/seek/history change, queued/count2/unchanged/null playbackKey. Deliberate subsequent Resume starts **A**, not H1 or B. Preserve playing/buffering/idle/error unchanged-current cases and queue-only Low/High/Next no-acquisition policy.
- Preserve first explicit app Play guarded exact-self **`requireUnselected:true`** admission with blank paused staging or exact live prepared Resume and terminal activation acceptance. System/headset/notification Play never claims output or imports a restored server queue. Selected unavailable remote remains selected, never stolen/fallback locally. Local and selected-remote Pause/Resume must dispatch to the intended receiver only, with no double audio/history.
