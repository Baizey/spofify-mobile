## Final user policy / one deliverable

User approved song caching, clarified **“yes its bound for stuff playing on this device, so it will not keep up with other playback devices”**, set **“the cap at 1 gb but otherwise cool”**, and accepted stable audio identity: **“we can largely assume streaming one song will stream the same song everytime”.**

Deliver one native local-playback cache with:

- **Current + next five occurrence steps**, not five total or five distinct tracks found by scanning farther.
- **At most 100 unique track IDs**, and **1GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes total**, including all owned media, incomplete/staging files, index/journals and temporary metadata. These are **non-crashing, non-playback-error eviction thresholds**, not reasons to fail playback or disable Play/Queue. No separate 64MiB track limit: an object that cannot fit the total budget streams uncached.
- FIFO eviction, except each **actual successful play/resume/new current** moves its cached track to the back, equivalent to remove+re-add. Cache hits, reads, prefetch and buffering callbacks do not promote.
- Persistent app-private cache and FIFO order across process restarts for the **same canonical origin + immutable accountId**, dormant until validated authentication. No cold-process or every-configure purge.

This is an app-managed **audio-only storage exception** to the endpoint's current `Cache-Control: no-store`, expressly approved by the user. `no-store` normally tells HTTP caches not to store; it is not an authentication restriction. Do not enable global OkHttp disk caching or change API/artwork caching, credentials, redirect or backup policies. Track IDs are assumed to identify stable audio; no strong-ETag contract, mandatory HEAD-before-hit, content-version proof or backend change is needed.

Prefetch improves next-track latency/jitter and can **increase bandwidth** when prefetched songs are skipped. Reusing retained songs reduces repeated-listen body bandwidth. Server/device-control, queue, authenticated session and lease availability are still required: caching is not offline playback authority, durable queue recovery or a fix for separately assigned BUG-103 connection-stuck analysis. No new cache settings/download UI or feature batch.

## Behavioral requirements

### Effective window, only on this receiver

Take at most five next **occurrence steps** from the actual accepted local traversal; deduplicate IDs within the window and against current without extending it. Example `A | B B C D E F`: prefetch B/C/D/E, not F. Repeat-one predicts only A; repeat-all may wrap for at most five steps; repeat-off stops at the end. High/Low/Next/context/shuffle/repeat/reorder/remove/Undo must affect the plan exactly as they affect actual playback; queue keys/history remain occurrence-specific even when byte storage is shared.

Derive a bounded immutable plan on the service thread. Reconcile after legitimate local Play/queue changes/current transitions, cancel obsolete jobs on semantic changes, and do not restart work on progress/heartbeats. Completed out-of-window tracks may remain until eviction. No collection refetch, library scan or queue mutation to fill a window.

Only a **legitimate selected local receiver** with a live prepared queue may prefetch. A phone controlling a remote, an unavailable selected remote, an unselected/restored display or a prepare/import stage does not download audio. Never register/acquire output to cache. An explicit local queue action while paused may prefetch without starting playback. Pause/Stop, authority/session loss or service destruction cancels speculative I/O; passive recovery/heartbeats do not revive it. No prefetch worker, foreground promotion or wake policy keeping an otherwise disposable service alive. Prefetch never prepares/starts/seeks the player, requests focus, earns observed history or alters queue/current/context/selection.

### FIFO, capacity and I/O

- Track identity is scoped origin/account + track ID, independent of queue occurrence ID. Persist opaque keys and FIFO order. New retention admission appends once. Actual successful Play/Resume/new-current execution promotes once when output genuinely succeeds; buffering transitions/repeated callbacks cannot mint new promotions. Use existing start/transition/operation identity to deduplicate, **not history qualification/reporting**. A successful resume can promote without a new history nonce; a failed start cannot.
- On admission of a **new unique track ID**, count +1 including its partial/reservation and prune the **oldest eligible unpinned whole track** as needed. A duplicate ID does not add another entry. A full cache must attempt FIFO eviction to admit new tracks, not permanently refuse new admissions. If the oldest is playing, evict the next eligible entry without changing others' relative FIFO order. Pins cover active readers/writer: never delete underneath them or stall the current stream.
- Account **actual written bytes incrementally in bounded chunks**, including partial/staging/index/journal overhead; Content-Length is only a hint/reservation input, never the sole accounting or safety check. Reserve before writes and evict before/during a fill as the budget approaches, not only after a whole song completes. Unknown length may fill incrementally or bypass; incorrect/oversized lengths cannot grow unbounded. If the item cannot fit, active pins prevent fitting, or I/O/disk-full prevents caching, stream demand uncached or skip/cancel prefetch without exceeding reservations. No whole-song heap allocation or cache-limit exception may escape to Media3/visible playback state.
- **One cancellable speculative transfer**, sequential in upcoming order, with a finite attempt bound (default30s) and streaming buffers. Demand streaming/seek always wins: yield/cancel speculation when current needs network or is loading/buffering. Held speculative spans must not block current playback; stream normally on contention. Failed speculation is skipped for the unchanged plan, not retried in a loop or exposed as stuck transport. No extra current-track download merely to complete its cache entry.
- Reuse complete objects across opens. Provisional ranges/chunks may serve their active operation, but cancelled/truncated/error fills are not complete cache hits. Validate response/range coverage and expected length before declaring completion; reject invalid206/Content-Range/416/error/redirect bodies. A seek-only partial demand stream need not become a retained full song. Remove abandoned partials when unpinned; never splice conflicting responses. Known resource changes, deletion or corrupt files can invalidate best-effort, without a new freshness request per hit.
- OS eviction, ENOSPC, corrupt index or cache I/O failure means **not cached** and ordinary authorized streaming fallback where possible: no visible Playback error from the cache, disabled Play/Queue, exception leak or false cached success. Genuine upstream authentication/media failures retain their existing truthful behavior; cache fallback cannot swallow them or bypass authorization. Bypass a repeatedly failing cache path rather than brick controls. Stop writers before partial cleanup; failed deletion remains owned, counted/accounted and inaccessible as a hit, with recoverable count/byte bookkeeping for safe cleanup/reopen. Do not pretend space was reclaimed. Runtime best-effort fallback does not waive developer artifact accounting or permit ignored failed deletion.

### Persistent identity, privacy and authority

Use app-private `cacheDir` storage, opaque resource/scope keys and a credential-free index containing only the minimum byte/completion/FIFO metadata. No persisted cookie/token/password, URL/private catalog path or song-title/artist inventory; no secret-bearing logs. Existing manifest/data-extraction rules exclude backup/device transfer. Current policy requires credential encryption, **not a bespoke media-encryption engine**; ordinary app-private/device storage protection is the approved minimal approach. Authenticated image disk cache remains disabled.

Persist bytes/order across Activity/service/process recreation. On restart, open no content to playback until authentication has been validated and matches exact canonical origin + immutable accountId; cache presence never installs auth or restores a queue. Same-account/origin restoration/configure does not purge. Logout, actual account/server switch, shared401 or expired/invalid session immediately fence cache access/cancel calls and wipe the retired namespace after active readers stop. A failed/offline restoration leaves bytes dormant, not authorized for offline use. Fence late writes by client/session/service incarnation; cleanup cannot delete a successor namespace. Surface/account failed cleanup rather than silently losing evidence or making old bytes accessible.

Every cache hit and subsequent read must still satisfy genuine current client/session/origin/lease/local-owner/approved-URL checks. A cache hit needs **no audio HEAD/GET solely for freshness** under the accepted stable-track-ID assumption. The current contract does not add a separate per-track access check for a cache hit; do not invent one as version proof. Preserve ordinary control/session requests and shared401 invalidation, local cookie/session expiry and conservative output-lease expiry. Unseen remote revocation has the same existing coordination/lease observation limits, not a new offline bypass or a claim of instantaneous detection. All misses/ranges use the unchanged secure client; auth/redirect failures never become stale-byte fallback.

## Tests-first acceptance / measured effects

Use synthetic loopback media with distinct known bytes, real authenticated client/data-source integration and production service/AppGraph seams. Assert actual body bytes/requests/consumed hashes plus bounded cache inventory—not just a mocked hit. Preserve authority/first-Play/lifecycle/history/frozen queue and transport regressions.

1. **Warm/reuse/restart:** cold local playback fetches only current+permitted next5; later prefetched/cached B plays exact B with zero additional audio-body bytes and no new freshness HEAD. Same authenticated account/origin after process recreation reuses bytes and FIFO order; pre-validation/offline restore cannot. Queue/session/lease control remains active. A held slow prefetch cannot starve the current stream.
2. **Traversal/no remote work:** exercise actual High/Low/Next/shuffle/repeat/reorder/remove/duplicates and plan cancellation. Assert expected IDs/window, no collection fetch, no new requests from benign progress. Remote-selected controller emits zero audio/prefetch and never acquires output. Staging/recovery/browse stays silent and prefetch creates zero playback/focus/FGS/history effects.
3. **FIFO/play promotion:** admit A/B/C, hit/read/prefetch A, insert D → A evicted. Admit A/B/C, successfully play/resume A, insert D → B evicted. New current/repeat promotes appropriately; buffering/duplicate callbacks/failed starts do not. History qualification need not occur for successful playback promotion. Persist/reopen to prove order survives.
4. **Hard bounds/failure:** fill the cache, admit a new track, observe oldest-eligible eviction and **actual playback continuing**, not just a rejected write. Test100/101 unique IDs including partial/reserved entries and duplicate +0, byte cap before count cap, incrementally growing/unknown/incorrect declared lengths, staging+journal overhead, pinned oldest/all capacity unavailable and an overlarge item streamed uncached. Eviction occurs before/during bounded writes; never exceed **1,000,000,000 owned bytes** or100 unique IDs through admission. OS removal/corruption/ENOSPC, cancellation/partial/range/truncation and failed cleanup yield safe misses/fallback with zero cache-origin Playback errors, disabled controls, phantom cached success or retry loop. Assert current byte consumption/progress and recoverable accounting after writers stop/reopen; retained failed-deletion bytes still consume budget. Use smaller injected fixture budgets plus production-constant assertions, not huge downloads.
5. **Security/lifetime:** same IDs on different accounts/origins cannot share bytes. Logout/shared401/account/server switch during hit/fill invalidates immediately and purges safely; stale completions cannot repopulate or delete successor storage. Expired session/lost authority/off-origin/unapproved URL fails even with a warm cache. No authenticated redirects; valid Range/seek bytes remain exact. Inspect files/index/logs for secrets/private paths; backup exclusions remain. Known changed/corrupt resource invalidates, but do not assert strong-version detection the user did not require.

Use stable resource checks: <=1 speculative transfer, <=5 future steps, bounded chunk memory/reservations, no repeated body fetch on reuse, no progress-driven request storm. Compare actual current-stream progress with uncached baseline under held speculative I/O; investigate regressions rather than raising limits. No arbitrary test-count goal, new benchmarking framework or review committee.
