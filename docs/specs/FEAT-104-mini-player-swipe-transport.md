## Acceptance contract

### Surface and gestures

- Scope recognition to the mini-player artwork/identity area, reclaiming the removed Next slot for identity. Keep Play/Pause a separate ≥48dp child/sibling target; a pointer starting there must not trigger expansion or skipping. No horizontal detector on the shell, screen, list, navigation, expanded sheet or seek bar.
- Left/right are physical directions, including under RTL. Issue the existing semantic Next/Previous operation, **not** a guessed queue index, track-ID lookup or synthetic seek. Previous retains transport's restart-current behavior when applicable, including the first occurrence. Repeat/shuffle/traversal behavior stays service-owned.
- Lock intent only after Android touch slop, distinguishing dominant horizontal from dominant upward motion; preserve the existing 1.4 direction ratio and 64dp release-distance threshold unless focused evidence justifies a change. Upward intent expands only; downward/diagonal intent must not become a skip later in the same gesture. One release can produce at most one action, never both expansion and transport.
- Distance-based commitment is sufficient: explicitly keep velocity non-authoritative. A short fast flick cannot bypass slop/distance; crossing the threshold several times or moving quickly cannot generate multiple skips. No fling/momentum traversal. Commit only on valid pointer release, never during movement.
- Cancel on pointer cancellation/disposal, multi-touch, child/other-consumer ownership, invalidated interaction or insufficient final displacement. Reversal below the commit distance cancels. Progress publications must not restart the detector or recapture the gesture identity. A consumed drag must not fall through to click/expand on release.
- Leave physical system-edge Back starts unconsumed for horizontal transport (the existing 24dp edge reserve is a minimum fallback; account for actual system gesture insets/host coordinates). Do not request system-gesture exclusion. Preserve vertical scrolling outside the mini-player, normal child taps, taps with below-slop jitter and Android Back.
- Preserve the full-width flat dock, existing progress line, inset/IME/chrome behavior and identity hierarchy. No extra persistent control row, cramped replacement buttons or unrelated motion/redesign.

### Transport truthfulness and stale work

- Gestures supplement existing guarded service/coordinator transport; they do not prepare/fetch/import a queue, connect/recover output to make a swipe valid, or call a raw player directly. No autoplay on startup, restoration, expansion, browsing or reconnection. An explicit valid swipe has the same playback intent as the corresponding existing transport action, including paused/buffering behavior.
- For horizontal transport, capture the current non-null `PlaybackInteractionKey` and available directions at **pointer down**. Use that captured identity through release and serialized dispatch. Do not simply call a freshly rebound `controls.next()`/`previous()` from `rememberUpdatedState` after a held gesture. Tap/upward expansion remains presentation-only and available whenever the item is shown, independently of transport key/capability availability.
- Recheck live session/client, coordinator/output/authority, current occurrence/queue generation and direction capability at dispatch, including after coordinator waits. Logout, same-account reauthentication, server/client/service replacement, release, output selection/transfer/unavailability, queue replacement or intervening current-occurrence changes invalidate the held swipe; no retargeting, delayed replay or remote-to-local fallback. Include unsampled A→B→A transitions, not merely equality of the latest UI DTO.
- Progress/elapsed, artwork, ordinary buffering and benign same-owner lease renewal alone must not invalidate a valid held swipe. Capability removal does invalidate that direction even if the interaction key remains stable (for example changed repeat/end conditions).
- No current item: no mini-player/actions. Empty/unprepared/disconnected/unknown capability: fail closed, no audio start or queue initialization. Next at a terminal boundary without permitted wrap is unavailable/no-op; do not falsely claim success. Previous/restart is not disabled merely because there is no prior item. Use real current transport capabilities, including repeat-all wrapping and explicit skips under repeat-one; do not infer availability solely from `upcoming.isNotEmpty()` or `queueIndex > 0`.
- Disabled/cancelled/stale actions must not emit success feedback/haptics or optimistic song changes. Preserve existing truthful errors/selected-device feedback; an unconfirmed remote command is not successful local playback and is never retried automatically. One committed swipe submits at most one transport command.

### Accessibility and discoverability

- Keep the identity's standard click action labeled **Expand Now Playing** and the independently focusable/state-labeled Play/Pause control. Add named custom accessibility actions **Next song** and **Previous song** on the mini-player identity, using the same capability/identity-guarded operations as touch. Omit unavailable custom actions (or expose a genuinely disabled standard equivalent); do not report an unavailable action as successfully performed. Revalidate captured identity when an accessibility callback executes.
- Expose concise swipe guidance in the mini-player's accessible context without replacing song/credit identity or spamming announcements on progress. Tap-to-expand remains the discoverable non-swipe path to visible Previous/Next controls; verify those remain reachable to touch, keyboard/Switch Access and TalkBack. No new persistent cramped buttons are required. Any optional visual teaching must be brief/non-layout-disrupting, not a new dock row.
- Retain complete accessible song/credit context, ≥48dp targets, clear focus/state and Unicode fallback. At 320dp width and font scale 2, title/credit must not overlap Play/Pause or clip vertically; allow the existing shared dock growth for larger text.
