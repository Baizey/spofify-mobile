## Remembering, identity and privacy acceptance

- Automatically replace the single separate Android Keystore-encrypted login record only after a **fully successful, still-current client/session authentication**, retaining exact origin, authenticated username and unmodified password. No form opt-in, Use/Forget UI or password-based auto-submit on launch. Never persist a failed, partial, cancelled, stale/retired-client or expired-auth attempt; delayed prior-client success/failure must not change newer state, saved login, busy/error or playback connection.
- When the login form first appears, the latest valid encrypted successful record supplies **all three fields together**, even if `connection.server`/`lastServer` was later overwritten by a failed attempt at another origin. Do not assemble saved username/password with another server's preference. Once the user edits, error/retry/recomposition must preserve those edits; returning to an old identity must not silently resurrect its cleared password. Without a valid record, use a credential-free address hint/empty form, not invented credentials.
- Canonical origin change (including scheme/host/port, or invalid address) or username edit clears any mismatched password and reveal state before submission; cosmetic same-origin edits need not clear. Do not attach remembered credentials to a new identity. Preserve raw password bytes/whitespace; keep secrets only in ephemeral in-process form/non-data redacted secret objects and encrypted storage, **never** saved-instance state, `rememberSaveable`, StateFlow, parcels, URLs, logs, plaintext preferences, backups/device transfer or screenshots except synthetic fixtures.
- Sign out still invalidates the session/authority and stops locally owned audio, but retains the latest encrypted successful login for the next form. Expiry similarly requires explicit re-authentication without credential loss. Existing cookie/session restoration and explicit offline retry stay unchanged: they are **not** password-autofill auto-login. No startup/restoration autoplay or registration/transport side effects from autofill.
- Safely migrate/remove legacy `remember_login` gating, including an existing **false** value; it must never silently suppress the next successful automatic save. Preserve valid encrypted records and original session wire format/namespace. Do not resurrect previously deleted credentials or touch unrelated preferences/keys. Corrupt/invalidated Keystore data fails closed to usable manual login without crash, plaintext fallback or broad key/cache deletion.
- If credential persistence fails **after successful current auth**, retain that valid signed-in session and report a brief truthful failure-to-save notice; do not claim the new credentials were remembered or replace the previously durable login with a failed attempt. Keep authenticated runtime identity separate from the old saved record. Storage/auth/network errors stay safe and concise; no raw backend bodies/secrets.
